Albert Einstein was a German-born theoretical physicist who developed the theory of relativity and made major contributions to modern physics.

He received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921 for his work on the photoelectric effect.

For more information, see [Albert Einstein's biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Einstein).
