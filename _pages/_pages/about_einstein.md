Albert Einstein was a German-born theoretical physicist who developed the theory of relativity, one of the two pillars of modern physics.

His work is also known for its influence on the philosophy of science. He is generally regarded as one of the most influential scientists of the twentieth century.

Einstein received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921 for his services to theoretical physics, and especially for his discovery of the law of the photoelectric effect.

For more information, see [Albert Einstein's biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Einstein).
